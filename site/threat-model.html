Threat model

Scope: the ledger CLI, its workspace directory, and the artefacts it emits. Out of scope: the employer's ATS, their mail provider, the recruiter's laptop after the workspace is copied, and anything the tool does not run.

What it cannot defend against

State this plainly in any procurement conversation.

  1. Wholesale rewriting. Anyone with write access to the workspace can delete send_log.jsonl, fabricate entries, recompute the chain, and produce a log that verifies. The chain proves internal consistency, not provenance. Closing this requires replicating the head hash off-host — emailed to the coordinator, committed to a separate repository, or written to an append-only service — and comparing it later. That is an operational control, not a feature of this binary.
  2. A hostile operator with root on the machine. They can patch the binary, read the PII, or alter files without leaving evidence the binary will notice.
  3. Email content after it leaves the tool. Delivery receipts, the candidate's inbox, and the mail provider's logs are outside this system.
  4. Truthfulness of the captured disposition. The tool records what a named decision maker selected. It cannot detect a decision maker selecting FIELD because it is faster.
  5. Timestamps. They are the operator machine's clock. There is no trusted time source and no monotonic clock guarantee beyond the ordering checks in FORMAT_SPEC.md §5.

Trust boundaries

ATS export (untrusted text) ──► importer ──► roster
form capture (semi-trusted)  ──► validation ──► dispositions ──┐
approved copy (signed)       ──► renderer ──► send log (chained)
operator (trusted, logged)   ──► quarantine / migrate ──► operator.jsonl
  • The ATS export is untrusted input: it is parsed defensively and fuzzed.
  • The form is semi-trusted: values are validated, but the choice itself is a human act.
  • Approved copy is trusted but versioned, so drift is visible.
  • The operator is trusted but logged: quarantine and migration write an operator note, and neither is silent.

Reporting

If you find a way to make the tool render an email for a blocked row, accept an edited log, or accept a torn tail without quarantine, that is a bug worth escalating before the pilot starts.
